# Adding your new signature in Outlook

1. Open **beenish-warraich-logo-top.html** (logo on top) or **beenish-warraich-logo-bottom.html** (logo at the bottom) by double-clicking it (it opens in your web browser).
2. Click anywhere on the page, press **Ctrl+A** (Mac: **Cmd+A**) to select everything, then **Ctrl+C** (Mac: **Cmd+C**) to copy.
3. Open the Outlook signature settings:
   - **New Outlook / Outlook on the web:** Settings (gear icon) > **Accounts** > **Signatures** (in some versions: Mail > Compose and reply).
   - **Classic Outlook (desktop):** **File** > **Options** > **Mail** > **Signatures...**
4. Create a new signature (or select your current one), click in the signature box, delete any old text, and press **Ctrl+V** (Mac: **Cmd+V**) to paste.
5. Set it as the default for **New messages** and **Replies/forwards**, then click **Save** (classic: **OK**).
6. Send yourself a test email to check that the logo and links look right.

Tip: If the logo shows up as a blank box, make sure Outlook is allowed to download pictures.
